Privacy Policy

My Cashbook
Effective Date: 11 March 2026

Overview: My Cashbook is designed with an offline-first architecture. All your daily financial entries, cash accounts, income, and expense records are stored locally on your device. We do not operate private backend servers that harvest, monitor, or track your personal financial records.

1. Information We Store Locally

The core functionality of the application runs entirely on your device. The following data is processed and stored on your local hardware:

  • Transaction entries (amounts, categories, notes, timestamps).
  • Custom categories and payment method labels.
  • App preferences (currency, theme, reminder times, language selection).

2. Google Sign-In & Google Drive Cloud Backup (Optional)

The app offers an optional cloud backup feature powered by Google Drive. If you choose to enable this feature, the app interacts with Google services as follows:

  • Google Account Authentication: When you connect your Google account, the app accesses basic profile identifiers (your email address and display name) strictly to authenticate you and display the active backup account in the app UI.
  • Google Drive AppData Folder (Scope: drive.appdata): Cloud backups are saved exclusively to your Google Drive's hidden Application Data folder. The app cannot see, access, modify, or delete any of your personal files, documents, or photos in Google Drive. It can only manage backup archives created by My Cashbook.
  • Zero Third-Party Transmission: Your backup database is transmitted directly and securely (via HTTPS/TLS) between your device and Google's servers. We never receive, store, or inspect your backup files.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
My Cashbook's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Local File Export & Sharing (Optional)

You can create manual backup files and save them to your internal device storage or share them via the standard Android share sheet. The app uses Android's native Storage Access Framework (SAF); it only accesses the specific file or destination folder that you explicitly select.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Start.io)

To keep My Cashbook free and support ongoing development, the app displays third-party advertisements served by Start.io (formerly StartApp).

When ads are displayed, Start.io's SDK may automatically collect and process certain technical device information, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID), Android ID, or vendor identifiers.
  • Network & Technical Details: IP address, mobile carrier, operating system version, device manufacturer, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Coarse Location Data: Country or regional location inferred from your IP address.
  • Ad Interaction Telemetry: Impressions, click data, and viewing timestamps.

This information is collected solely by Start.io to serve relevant, fraud-protected, and frequency-capped advertisements. We do not sell your personal cashbook entries or share your financial records with Start.io.

5. Data Security

  • In Transit: All communications with Google Drive and Firebase/AdMob use strict HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • At Rest: Local data is sandboxed by the Android operating system, preventing other apps from accessing your cashbook database.

6. Children’s Privacy

My Cashbook is not targeted at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Deletion & Retention Policy

Users have complete control over their data at all times. Below are the steps you can take to delete your information immediately:

How to Delete Your Data

  1. Delete Cloud Backups (Google Drive):
    • Open My Cashbook.
    • Go to the Left Menu (or Settings) → tap Backup & Restore.
    • Tap Cloud Backups.
    • Tap the Delete (Trash) icon next to any backup snapshot to permanently remove it from Google Drive.
    • Tap Disconnect / Sign Out to remove your Google account credentials from the app.
  2. Delete Local Device Data:
    • Go to Android Settings → Apps → My Cashbook → Storage → Clear Data / Clear Storage (or uninstall the app). This permanently deletes all local transactions and application configurations.
  3. Revoke Google Drive Permissions:
    • You can revoke the app's authorization at any time by visiting your Google Account Third-Party Permissions page and selecting My Cashbook → Remove Access.
  4. Submit a Deletion Request via Email:
    • If you have any questions or require support regarding data removal, you can email us directly at: ashokrawat.dev.apps@gmail.com.

Data Retention Schedule
